The boundary

Clear boundaries make the work easier to judge.

What is not evidenced stays clearly named—so public examples can speak for themselves.

  • No production RAG claim is made.
  • No location, employer, client, compensation, or language-proficiency details are asserted.
  • No arbitrary progress percentages or years-of-experience figures are used.
  • Historical LinkedIn-sync material is context, not the source of truth.
